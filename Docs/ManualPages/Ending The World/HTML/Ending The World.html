On the Subject of Ending the World

"Sir... We are at launch! Turn your key!" — Steve

  1. Press the button to hear the launch code.
  2. Listen carefully.
  3. Look up each word and noise pair, to identify the code.
  4. Enter the authorisation code on the digit display.
  5. Turn the key to end the world.
